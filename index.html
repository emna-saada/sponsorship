01 · Who we are

Students who tell engineering stories.

The MSE Press Club is the media and storytelling club of the Manouba School of Engineering. We cover the events, the people and the ideas that shape our young school, and we share them with everyone, not just engineers.

We have built our community on Instagram and Facebook with event coverage, interviews and behind-the-scenes content. Now we are taking the next step: a podcast that brings experts, students and international guests to one table, and to a much wider audience.

02 · Our school

The first public engineering school in Tunisia taught fully in English.

The Manouba School of Engineering (MSE), University of Manouba, opened its doors in 2022 with a bold idea: train engineers for the data-driven, climate-aware world, entirely in English.

Lens 01🛰️

Geoinformatics

The science of collecting, analysing and mapping information about places: roads, buildings, land and water, and how the earth's surface changes over time, using satellites, drones and maps.

  • Earth observation
  • Remote sensing
  • GIS
  • Territory & infrastructure
  • Spatial AI

Eco lens

Lens 02🌿

Ecological Informatics

The science of using data and computing to understand living systems: forests, wetlands and wildlife, and how ecosystems respond to pressure such as climate change or land use.

  • Biodiversity data
  • Ecosystem modelling
  • Environmental sensors
  • Climate
  • Eco-AI

Where they meet → Both work with data tied to a place. One tells us what is on the ground and how it changes; the other tells us how the living systems on that ground are doing. That overlap is the thesis of our whole podcast.
